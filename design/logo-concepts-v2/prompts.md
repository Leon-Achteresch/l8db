# l8db App-Logo-Prompts

Erstellt mit der integrierten Bildgenerierung. Die konkrete Modellversion ist in diesem Werkzeug nicht wählbar.

## 01 Navy Glass

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: finished square app icon for l8db, a modern desktop database client. Create a premium icon on a deep midnight-navy rounded-square tile. At the center, render the exact characters "l8" as a single bold, impeccably legible lowercase-l plus digit-eight monogram (not "18", not "L8", no other letters). Integrate a clear database symbol into the mark: three stacked cylindrical database layers immediately beneath/behind the l8, still readable at 32 px. Restrained glasmorphism: one translucent smoked-glass layer, precise luminous cyan edge highlights, subtle depth, almost no blur. Strong silhouette, balanced spacing, polished geometry, contemporary software brand quality. Icon only, straight-on view, no perspective mockup, no label, no wordmark, no border, no extra text, no busy reflections, no photographic scenery.

## 02 Frosted Light

The exact lowercase characters "l8" must be large, bold, unambiguous and readable at 32 px. Behind the l8, integrate a clearly recognizable database cylinder made from two broad elliptical stacked disks in pale teal frosted glass, with restrained graphite outlines. Crisp UI-brand geometry, subtle translucency and soft ambient shadow, very little glow, no heavy gradients. Front view, precise edges and generous breathing room. Icon only: no words, no 'db', no other numbers or letters, no mockup, no watermark, no surrounding scene.

## 03 Glass Eight

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a premium square desktop app icon for l8db, a database client. Create a simple full-bleed icon: EVERY PIXEL of the square canvas has an opaque uniform deep emerald background, including all four corners; there is no rounded-square tile, no transparent area, no outer shadow and no edge glow. Center a large perfectly readable lowercase 'l8' monogram in pale mint satin glass. The '8' is subtly built from two stacked oval database volumes, with two clear horizontal separation lines so it reads as a database tool. Crisp front-on 2.5D style, controlled narrow highlights, elegant and contemporary, generous margins, legible at 32 px. Exact text only 'l8'; no extra letters, words, DB, labels, borders, mockup, scenery, or watermark.

## 04 Violet Stack

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a finished square app icon for l8db, a desktop database client. Design direction: matte indigo-violet with restrained glass. Fill the ENTIRE square canvas, including all four corners, with one solid opaque dark indigo color; no transparency, no rounded tile, no outer shadow or glow. In the middle place a simple front-facing three-layer database cylinder made of frosted lavender glass, with clearly separated horizontal bands. Put the exact lowercase characters "l8" on the cylinder face in large crisp near-white geometric lettering; lowercase l with a subtle foot, then numeral 8. The l8 must remain legible at 32 px. Use only delicate glass edge highlights and modest depth, no neon, no extra panel or frame. Balanced icon geometry, generous margins. No text other than l8, no 'db', no labels, no mockup, no watermark.

## 05 Copper Cutout

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: polished square app icon for l8db, a desktop database management tool. Design direction 5: sophisticated warm graphite and copper, simple and compact. Build a clear three-tier database cylinder as the main symbol, with the exact lowercase characters "l8" cut out in large negative space across its front face; the two glyphs must be unmistakably readable at 32 px. The cylinder appears as satin dark metal with a single lightly frosted glass top surface and thin muted copper seams. Background is a uniform charcoal rounded-square tile; no nested frames. Minimal controlled specular highlights, restrained depth, no neon and no bloom. Symmetrical straight-on composition, strong silhouette and ample margins. No other lettering, no 'db', no extra digits, no labels, no mockup, no watermark, no scenery.
